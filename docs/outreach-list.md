# Liftly — Coach & Lifter Outreach List

**Prepared:** 2026-07-06. Companion to `docs/gtm-strategy.md` §5–6.
**How to use:** work top-down within a tier, 10 DMs/day max from the founder's personal handle (not the brand — the June research showed lifters answer *people*). Log every thread in a sheet: handle, date, reply?, stage, next action. Follower counts are approximate and move; verify handle + activity before each DM. Names below are real, well-known figures in the niche — the *fit rationale* is our assessment, not a claim about them.

**The offer, per tier:**
- **Coaches:** free console until 5 clients + white-glove migration of their Sheets, then $20/seat. Referral code: their followers get 30-day Pro trial, they get 20% of year-one revenue from athletes who sign up without a coach.
- **Lifters/creators:** free Pro for a year + a genuinely good form-check review of their own clip, no posting obligation. If they like it, a coach-in-bio-style referral code.

**Scaling this tier past named leads (the real volume is the long tail):**
1. **IG hashtag mining:** #powerliftingcoach, #onlinepowerliftingcoach, #powerliftingcoaching — filter to accounts 1k–20k followers with "DM for coaching" in bio. Expect 30–50 qualified handles/hour of mining.
2. **OpenPowerlifting → coach discovery:** pull mid-level meet results (totals 400–700kg men / 250–450kg women), find the lifters on IG, check who they tag as coach ("@X handles my programming"). These coaches demonstrably have paying rosters.
3. **r/powerlifting coaching megathreads + USAPL club directory:** coaches self-identify; club coaches often coach remotely on the side.
4. **TrueCoach/CoachRx refugees:** search X/Reddit for "TrueCoach 5% fee" complaints since their Jan 2026 fee change — priced-out coaches are shopping right now.

## DM scripts (from the June playbook — what already got replies)

**Tier A coach, cold:**
> Hey [name] — saw your client [athlete]'s [lift] PR post. I'm building Liftly (AI first-pass for powerlifting coaches — it watches the videos, reads RPE trends, drafts the week's adjustments, and *you* approve everything before it ships). I'm looking for 5 founding coaches: free until your 6th client, and I'll migrate your sheets myself. Worst case you lose 20 minutes on a call with a fellow lifting nerd. Interested?

**Tier B lifter, cold (mirror the research openers — they worked):**
> Hey — that [weight] [lift] was clean. I build an AI form-check tool and ran your clip through it out of curiosity (bar path + where the concentric slowed). Want the breakdown? Free, no catch — if it's wrong, tell me why and that's worth more to me than a signup.

**Tier C brand:**
> [Name] — we built [their concept: RPE autoregulation / block periodization / velocity-loss RIR] into an adaptive engine and published the methodology. Before we scale it, I want the person who [defined the concept] to tear it apart. 20-minute call or async doc, your call. Happy to pay for your time.

**Follow-up cadence:** one bump at day 4 ("no worries if not — one-line version: [offer]"), then stop. This community is small; being pushy once is being pushy everywhere.

**Tracking:** add `?ref=<handle>` to every link sent (GTM Phase 0 instrumentation) so replies → signups → retention is measurable per person.
